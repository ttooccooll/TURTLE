TURTLE - Word Puzzle Game

How to Play

Guess the TURTLE in six tries.

After each guess, the color of the tiles will change to show how close your guess was to the word.

Frequently Asked Questions


What makes TURTLE different from other word games?

TURTLE was built with privacy at its core. Unlike other popular word games that collect user data for advertising purposes, TURTLE follows a transparent business model: one free game per day, with additional plays available through small Bitcoin Lightning payments. No tracking, no ads, no data collection (beyond a voluntary username that you can makeup).

Does TURTLE collect my personal data?

No! TURTLE was specifically designed to avoid collecting user data. We believe games should respect your privacy.

What is the Bitcoin Lightning Network?

The Bitcoin Lightning Network is a second-layer solution built on top of the Bitcoin blockchain that enables fast, low-cost micropayments. It allows you to make small payments quickly without the typical transaction fees associated with regular Bitcoin transactions.

How many languages does TURTLE support?

TURTLE currently supports 14 languages: English, French, German, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Afrikaans, Swahili, Turkish, Vietnamese, Xhosa, and Zulu. We're continually working to add more languages to make the game accessible to players worldwide.
